Accountability in the Apps We Use

The digital world wasn’t built with kids in mind. We’re here to change that — starting with transparency, education, and a clear vision for what comes next.

Where We Are Today

Right now, every major app — Instagram, Snapchat, TikTok, Discord, Spotify, Roblox, YouTube and more — allows a child to create an adult account, even when:

  • The device is set up as a child account
  • The operating system knows the child’s real age
  • The parent approved the download through Apple Family Sharing or Android Family Link

Apps rely on self‑reported age, which kids can bypass in seconds. Parents assume protections are in place. Kids assume they’re safe. Apps assume nothing. This is the accountability gap.

What Parents Need to Know

Behind the Scenes

  • Apps do not automatically switch to teen mode, even on child devices.
  • Apps do not read the child’s real age from Apple or Android.
  • Risky features are often enabled by default: DMs, location sharing, chat, discovery.
  • Parents are rarely notified when these features turn on.

Why It Matters

Our teen survey shows kids access major platforms between ages 8–13, long before most parents expect. That’s why ReadyOnline focuses on helping families configure, clean up, and stay aware of the digital spaces their kids use.
