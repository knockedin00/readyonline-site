Our Mission

ReadyOnline helps youth and families build digital reflexes — the “that was a close call” awareness that keeps you safe online. No fear. No judgment. No shame. Just skills, confidence, and support when you need it.

“Being online is like learning to drive. It’s exciting, empowering, and full of possibilities — but it requires awareness and training. We help you build the ‘close call’ reflexes that keep you safe, without making you afraid of the road.”

— Charlie Kropf, Co‑Founder

What You’ll Learn

🧠 Spot Red Flags

Recognize manipulation, pressure, scams, and risky situations early.

🔒 Stay in Control

Build habits that keep your accounts, identity, and privacy safe.

🆘 Course‑Correct Anytime

Know what to do — and who to call — when something feels off.

🤝 Support Friends

Learn how to help someone who’s struggling online.

Our Shame‑Free Philosophy

Victims are never to blame. Shame keeps people silent, and silence keeps people unsafe. We’re here to break both.

If something goes wrong online, you didn’t “mess up.” You were targeted by someone who knew exactly what they were doing. The shame belongs to them — not you.

Continuous Training

Whether you’re new to digital life or already online every day, ReadyOnline gives you ongoing training to stay aware, confident, and prepared. Digital skills aren’t one‑and‑done — they grow with you.

About: ReadyOnline empowers youth and families with continuous digital safety training. We teach awareness, reflexes, and confidence to navigate online life responsibly — without fear or shame.
