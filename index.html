The ReadyOnline model

Pause · Think · Act

Pause — interrupt the automatic response.
Think — check what is really happening.
Act — use a pre-rehearsed move.

Not a parental control. Not a one-time course. Practice that stays with you.

Mission + values

Practice beats panic.
Instincts beat rules.

Real stories, simulations, and useful moves help families stay connected without shame or blame.

Whole family

Kids, teens, adults, and seniors learn the same timeless patterns in ways that fit their real lives.

Real-world practice

Every scenario ends with a debrief: what happened, why it worked, and what the move is.

Confidence, not fear

Safety is not about shrinking someone’s world. It is about giving them more choices in it.

The No Shame Zone

Something happening online is never a reason to go through it alone.

No “you should have known better.” No automatic punishment. ReadyOnline helps families make a clear promise: stay calm, listen first, and solve the next step together.

What you’ll learn

The skills that protect your real digital life.

These are the six skills teens and adults identified as most useful to practice.

01

Privacy + personal information

Know what an app, post, or stranger can learn from what you share.

02

Money + account protection

Protect passwords, verification codes, financial accounts, and identity.

03

Healthy digital habits

Recognize when design, pressure, or FOMO is shaping your choices.

04

Scams + fraud

Spot urgency, excitement, and fear of missing out before they make the decision for you.

05

AI + content awareness

Practice evaluating deepfakes, altered media, and claims that demand a fast reaction.

06

Relationships + support

Navigate pressure, manipulation, and the moments a friend needs a calm person beside them.
