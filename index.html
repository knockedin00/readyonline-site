ReadyOnline

Basic digital safety skills for texts, chats and social apps—so instincts grow before the pressure hits.

This is a skills app, not monitoring. Think “learning to cross the street” for the digital world.

What You’ll Learn
Short, repeatable trainings that feel like phishing drills for the apps you already use.

Spot Red Flags in Chats

Learn the patterns scammers use in texts, DMs and game chats—before urgency takes over.

Move Safely Between Apps

Know when it’s okay to switch apps and when “let’s talk on Snap/Discord instead” is a warning sign.

Protect Accounts & Info

Keep passwords, codes and personal details out of the hands of people who shouldn’t have them.

Handle Pressure & Manipulation

Recognize guilt, fear and FOMO tactics—and practice saying no without freezing.

Support Friends Safely

Know what to do if a friend is being pushed for photos, money or secrets online.

Report, Block & Recover

Practice the steps to take after a bad click or message, instead of hiding it.

Like Learning to Cross the Street

Toddlers start by holding a parent’s hand. Over time, looking both ways and choosing the safest path becomes instinct. ReadyOnline does the same for texts and apps.

Step 1 · With Guidance
Practice spotting safe vs. risky messages together, with clear “stop, check, tell” rules.
Step 2 · In Familiar Places
Apply the same skills in school email, group chats and the apps you already use.
Step 3 · Instinct Everywhere
Over time, pausing, questioning and verifying becomes automatic—like avoiding a dark alley.
For Families & Schools

Parents and school admins can assign modules based on the apps students use, then review progress and conversation guides together.

  • Check the apps
    Select Instagram, Snapchat, Discord, Roblox, email and more for each student.
  • Assign trainings
    Match skills modules to those apps—texts/chats, follows & DMs, scams, pressure and recovery.
  • Talk it through
    Use built‑in discussion prompts so students know they won’t be judged for asking for help.
