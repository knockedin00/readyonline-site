Digital reflexes for a lifetime online.

ReadyOnline helps families and youth recognize digital red flags — from manipulation and scams to subtle social pressure. We teach awareness and reflexes that make safe choices automatic, not reactive.

Vision

We’re building the world’s first continuous digital‑resilience app — a place where families learn, practice, and track digital habits together. Technology provides guardrails, but it can’t stay ahead of every bad actor. Instincts fill that gap — and the skills built early flex into adulthood in all stages of life, shaping confident digital behavior at work and in personal life.

How it works

ReadyOnline combines short lessons, real‑world scenarios, and reflection prompts to build digital instincts over time. It’s not about perfect behavior — it’s about learning to pause, look both ways, and choose what’s safe and healthy.

Help us shape the app teens, parents, and schools actually want.
“Being online should feel like crossing the street — you pause, look both ways, and trust your instincts. Technology can help, but it can’t replace awareness. We teach those reflexes early, so staying safe becomes automatic.”
Charlie Kropf · Co‑Founder

No shame. Just learning.

Learning digital safety should never involve fear or embarrassment. Mistakes are part of growth — and every “close call” is a chance to build stronger reflexes. ReadyOnline is built on calm guidance, shared learning, and support without judgment.

Accountability · Apps & People

Digital safety isn’t just about restrictions — it’s everyone’s responsibility. Kids want to learn these skills and often wish they had them when they first explored social media. We can’t depend on apps to self‑monitor for everyone’s benefit. Education is key, and together we can hold companies accountable to create safer digital environments for all users.

Education + shared responsibility + platform accountability
ReadyOnline sits between families and platforms — helping youth build instincts while we push for clearer guardrails on child accounts. We believe safety is a shared effort: parents, schools, apps, and young people all play a role in making online spaces safer and more honest.
