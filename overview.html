How ReadyOnline Works

ReadyOnline teaches families how to build digital instincts — the same way we learn real‑world safety. Kids, teens, parents, and seniors all move through the same four stages: Guidance → Practice → Confidence → Independence.

The Four Stages

Guidance

Adults model calm, clear digital behavior and explain what’s safe and what’s not.

Practice

Kids and teens try things with support — not restriction — so instincts can form.

Confidence

They begin to recognize red flags, pressure, and manipulation on their own.

Independence

They navigate digital spaces with clarity, calm, and strong instincts.

Reading the Digital Environment

Just like crossing a street, digital safety is about reading the environment: what feels normal, what feels off, and when to pause or ask for help.

For Seniors

ReadyOnline includes simple, clear modules for older adults who want to stay connected without feeling overwhelmed by scams, pressure, or confusing technology.
