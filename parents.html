What teens say

Connection is the safety feature.

Teen and adult survey results show where a calmer response can make the biggest difference.

80%

need to know they will not get in trouble

Make the no-punishment promise before your family needs it.

42%

worry about losing phone access

Phone confiscation can turn a request for help into a reason to stay silent.

36%

say adults overreact when they ask for help

Listen first. The first response decides whether the door stays open.
