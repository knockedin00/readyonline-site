What You Can Do

Right now, you can assign modules and use the Follow‑Up Guides — all without creating an account or sharing any personal data. In our upcoming app, you’ll have a Family Dashboard where you’ll be able to add profiles, track progress, and receive updated trainings.

Assign Modules

Choose modules based on your child’s apps or interests. Build instincts for the environments they use most.

Follow‑Up Guides

Use simple conversation prompts to reinforce instincts and confidence.

What I Wish I Knew

Teens wish they’d learned how to spot scams, protect privacy, and recognize manipulation before they got their first smartphones.

Skills I Want Now

Identity fraud awareness, protecting privacy & financial accounts, and how to build healthy digital habits.

When I Ask for Help

Teens feel safest when adults listen calmly, don’t judge, and know their conversation will remain private.

What Seniors/Retirees Need

Seniors are a targeted demographic — not because they’re naïve, but because they’re trusted, experienced, and financially stable. Modern scams use tools that didn’t exist when they were working. These trainings help them stay independent and in control.

Spot Modern Scams

Learn how today’s fraudsters use technology — from fake tech‑support calls to AI‑generated voices — and how to verify what’s real.

Protect Accounts & Devices

Understand how to secure online banking, email, and smartphones against unauthorized access and data theft.

Stay Confident Online

Build comfort using technology while keeping privacy intact — learning digital instincts that preserve independence.
