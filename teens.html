No Shame Zone

Getting tricked does not mean you are careless.

Pressure works because it is designed to work. If something online makes you feel scared, ashamed, rushed, or trapped, you deserve a person who stays calm, listens first, and helps you make a plan.

If this happens, do this

Someone wants to move the chat.

“My account gets checked. Move to this private app. Don’t tell anyone yet.” Secrecy and a rush to move platforms are signals to slow down—not to prove you are being nice.

Red flags by app

Different apps. Same patterns.

Suspicious does not mean you have to prove something is wrong. It means you slow down and protect your choices.

INSTAGRAM + SNAPCHAT

Perfect profiles + disappearing pressure

New accounts with no history, Snap Map adds, private-story requests, fake opportunities, or a streak used to make you feel guilty.

TIKTOK + YOUTUBE

Creator access + fast claims

“Collab” offers that need account information, influencer impersonation, or content that demands an instant reaction.

DISCORD + ROBLOX

Private servers + obligation

Nitro gifts, Robux codes, files to download, screen-share requests, or “just us” private-server invitations.

SPOTIFY SOCIAL PRESSURE

A playlist can be social pressure too.

A shared playlist, group chat, or “everyone is listening” feeling should not force you to reveal more, join a private space, or stay in a situation that feels wrong.

AI + DEEPFAKES

When a fake looks real

Check context first: why am I receiving this, who benefits if I believe it, and does it create urgency or FOMO?

SUPPORT A FRIEND

Be the calm person.

Listen without blame. Save evidence if it is safe. Help them reach a trusted adult, counselor, or 988 when they feel scared or trapped.

Keep practicing

What to say when you need help.

You do not need the perfect words to start a hard conversation.

A trusted adult can be a parent, coach, teacher, counselor, aunt, uncle, or older sibling—someone who will stay calm and help you think.
Save the messages if you can do so safely. You do not need to respond, explain, or handle it alone.
If you feel scared, ashamed, or trapped because of something online, 988 is available by call or text 24/7. You do not have to explain everything to reach out.
