ReadyOnline

Skills for Real‑World Digital Life

You already know how to use apps — ReadyOnline helps you master how to read them. It’s about instincts: spotting scams, pressure, and manipulation before they happen. These skills make you stronger online and in real life.

Top Skills Teens Want to Learn

Identify Scams & Fraud

Learn how fake links, giveaways, and impersonations really work.

Protect Personal Info

Understand how data is collected and how to keep yours safe.

Spot Manipulation

Recognize pressure tactics, peer influence, and emotional triggers.

Recognize AI Content

Know when something’s been generated or altered — and why it matters.

Support Friends

Learn how to help someone who’s in a risky or uncomfortable situation online.

Prepare for Adult Scams

Get ready for college and workplace scams before they find you.

Confidence Levels

Most teens feel “somewhat confident” navigating online risks — but not enough to go it alone. ReadyOnline builds that confidence through practice, not lectures.
